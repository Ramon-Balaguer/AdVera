Every meeting, verifiable knowledge.

AdVera is built around one idea: everything is recorded, and everything can be checked. Every summary, decision, action and answer links back to the exact second of audio that supports it.

Capture

Three ways in.

Record from the browser with nothing to install, let the desktop agent capture your calls, or import the recordings you already have.

  1. 01

    From the browser

    Record the meeting with your microphone, right in the application. No plugin, no extension.

  2. 02

    Desktop agent

    On Windows, a small agent records your microphone and the sound of the call as separate tracks and sends them to your server.

  3. 03

    Import recordings

    Upload an audio or video file you already have. AdVera extracts the audio, transcribes it and adds it to the Brain like any other meeting.

Transcription

Who said what, and when.

Whisper transcribes the recording in around 100 languages, each part in the language it was spoken. Diarization tells the voices apart, and you name each one once: from then on the name is used everywhere.

Every line keeps its time. That time is what later turns a claim into something you can check.

What is recorded

Everything is recorded. Everything can be checked.

AdVera keeps the audio, the transcript with who said what and when, your notes, and what the AI concluded from them. Nothing is a black box.

The audio
Recordings keep their original tracks, microphone and system sound apart. An imported file keeps its audio.
The transcript
One definitive transcript per meeting, with the speaker, the language and the time of every segment.
Your notes
A Markdown editor per meeting, with references to other meetings. Notes are cited like the transcript.
What the AI did
Every call to the model is stored with its prompt version and raw answer. An item without a valid citation is dropped, not shown.

Cited answers

The answer, and the second it was said.

Ask anything about your meetings. The answer comes with its sources, and each source plays the audio at the moment it supports. When the meetings do not hold the answer, AdVera says so.

The Brain

One memory of every meeting you have had.

AdVera transcribes in around 100 languages, tells who is speaking and writes a summary of each meeting. The Brain connects all of it.

Search
Hybrid full-text and semantic search across all meetings, with cited answers.
Concept graph
People, projects, products and topics, and how they relate, drawn from what was said.
Timelines
How a project or a topic evolved, meeting after meeting, newest first.
Decisions and actions
Every decision, action, open question and risk of every meeting, filtered by state, owner, tag or date.

“The definitive transcript is the source of truth; the audio is its origin.”

Yours

It runs on your machines.

Your audio, transcripts and notes never leave your servers. The only thing that leaves the application is the text sent to the language-model server you choose: Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible server such as llama.cpp, llama-swap or vLLM.

How it works

From a conversation to knowledge you can check.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Record from the browser, or from the Windows desktop agent with microphone and system sound as separate tracks. Or import a file.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Whisper turns the audio into a definitive transcript, with the language of every chunk and who is speaking.

  3. 03

    Summarise

    Your language model extracts the summary, decisions, actions, questions, risks and concepts, each with its citations.

  4. 04

    Remember

    The Brain indexes it with the rest of your meetings: search, graph, timelines and every decision in one place.

Install

Up and running with one command.

AdVera runs with Docker Compose: PostgreSQL with pgvector, Redis, the API, three workers and the web application. The first time, a short wizard asks for the language and your model server. You can change all of it later in Settings.

1. Get the code

git clone https://github.com/your-org/advera.git
cd advera

2. Start it (CPU)

docker compose -f docker/compose.dev.yml up -d --build --wait

Or with an NVIDIA GPU

docker compose -f docker/compose.dev.yml -f docker/compose.nvidia.yml up -d --build --wait

Then open http://localhost:5173.

What you need

Software
Docker with Compose v2. For a GPU, the NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
A model server
Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible server, reachable from the containers, with JSON-schema output and a large context window.
Transcription
It runs on CPU. An NVIDIA GPU makes it much faster and more accurate; on our test machine the workers use about 5 GB of VRAM.
Disk
About 11 GB for the models, plus your audio.
Network
Internet on the first start to download the models. Then it works on your local network.

AdVera has no authentication yet: it is meant for one user on a local network. Do not expose it to the internet.
